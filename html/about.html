About Liberty Electronics

Liberty Electronics is a modern mobile shopping website designed to provide users with the latest smartphones, premium brands, and an easy online shopping experience. This project is developed by two students of Aptech Institute.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide customers with a fast, reliable and modern way to explore and buy smartphones from top brands such as Samsung, Apple, Pixel, Oppo, Realme and Xiaomi.

Why Choose Us?

Liberty Electronics focuses on clean UI design, detailed product information, and user-friendly features like sorting, filtering, cart system and product pages for every phone model.

Project Vision

Our goal is to create a professional e-commerce style platform that looks modern and premium while being responsive on all devices. This project represents our web development skills learned at Aptech Institute.
